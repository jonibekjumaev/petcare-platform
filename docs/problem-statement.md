# Problem Statement

**PetCare Platform** — a pet supplies store with per-pet profiles and an AI care assistant

---

## Target customer

Someone who works a 9-to-5 office job and looks forward to spending time with their
pet in the evening — but whose schedule leaves little room to think carefully about
that pet's nutrition and health.

## The problem

These owners want to care for their pets well, but a demanding schedule pushes them
toward fast, uninformed choices. Much as a busy person defaults to convenient food
for themselves, they pick pet products without really knowing what their animal
needs. Placing an order is easy. Ordering the *right* thing is a different matter.

## What exists today, and why it falls short

Conventional online pet stores provide a catalog and a checkout, and stop there. The
customer searches, the customer decides, and the store offers no guidance about what
actually suits their animal. The predictable result: customers choose on price and
buy wherever is cheapest that week. There is nothing to come back for.

## Our approach

Each customer registers a profile for their pet — species, breed, age, weight, and
any notes. The catalog filters to products appropriate for that animal, and the
system tracks what the customer has ordered over time.

An AI assistant answers questions using that specific pet's profile and the
customer's actual order history as context. Not a generic help chat — advice
grounded in this customer's animal and what they have already bought.

## What success looks like

Customers return because of the personalized experience rather than the price tag —
producing repeat purchases and durable customer loyalty. That is what separates this
from a market where the cheapest listing always wins.
